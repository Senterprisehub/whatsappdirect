WA Direct is a lightweight, installable web app (PWA) that lets you message anyone on WhatsApp without adding them to your contacts first. It's built for speed, privacy, and control — everything runs in your browser, and all data stays on your device.

Core Features

Send without saving
Enter any phone number with a country code, type your message, and tap Open WhatsApp. The chosen app launches directly with the number and text pre-filled. No contact book clutter, no "who is this?" moments.

Choose your WhatsApp app
Set your preferred target once in Settings — Official WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, WhatsApp Web (api.whatsapp.com), whatsapp:// scheme, or any popular clone like GB WhatsApp, FM WhatsApp, Yo WhatsApp, OG WhatsApp, Aero, MB, NS, or JT. A custom Android package name option covers everything else.

Auto-saving history
Every message you open is automatically stored in History. Browse, search by name/number/message, resend, edit, copy the number, or delete entries. Timestamps show today, yesterday, or the date.

Local contacts
Save a name for any number — it's stored only on your device, no cloud, no sync. The new Contacts tab lists all saved names, lets you tap one to prefill the compose screen, and lets you delete contacts individually. Names appear on History entries too, so you recognize who you messaged.

Premium UI
WhatsApp-inspired design with a deep emerald gradient header, layered card shadows, animated tab indicators, rounded avatars, and a smooth dark mode that follows your system or locks to your choice. Every tap has a subtle haptic-style animation.

CSV import / export
Back up your entire history as a CSV file with id, name, number, message, app, createdAt, count. Edit it in Excel or Google Sheets and re-import. Imported rows with names automatically populate your Contacts tab.

Install as an app
Install directly from your browser on Android, iOS, or desktop. The app generates its own icon and manifest, runs fullscreen with no browser chrome, and works offline for compose and history.

Persistent settings
Country, preferred app, theme, custom package name, and confirm-before-delete are all saved to localStorage. They survive reboots, restarts, power-offs, and app updates — no login required.

Privacy First

· No accounts, no servers, no analytics
· All data lives in your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB
· Nothing ever leaves your device unless you tap Open WhatsApp

Who It's For

· Sales and support teams messaging leads without saving every number
· Anyone who's tired of adding temporary contacts just to send one message
· Power users of WhatsApp clones who want one tool to route through any of them
· Privacy-conscious users who want zero cloud footprint
